MK-style platinum guide

A guide, written like one. Chapters in order, prose for the how, tables for the list. You find your place by reading, the way you would in a PowerPyx roadmap, and the route is implied by the sequence of sections rather than handed to you as steps.

read this first

Before you press start

4 missables in this list. "Merciless" and "There Are No Flukes" lock to your first story run. "Tower Time" is seasonal. "Two for One" needs a partner before the tower season rotates. If you start here, you protect all four.

Two runs total. Run one is the story on normal with the missables watched. Run two is cleanup: Krypt, towers, fatalities, grind. Nothing in the Krypt can be missed, so it can wait. The estimate for a clean v1 run is around 34 hours.

chapters 1 to 12

Story run

Play the story straight through on normal. The only thing to actively chase is a clean final fight: do not lose a round against the last boss, or "There Are No Flukes" is gone for that run. Difficulty can drop after chapter 3 without costing anything else.

TrophyRequirement
Blood SportWin one versus match. You get this in chapter 2's fights naturally.
Ten-Piece SetLand a 10-hit kombo. Scorpion's chapter 3 combo trial chains into versus.
Merciless MISSABLEFinish the story without dropping difficulty.
There Are No Flukes MISSABLEBeat the final boss without losing a round.
Chapter and VerseAll 12 chapters done. Pops with the credits.

after the credits

The Krypt

No missables, so it waited until now. Open the Krypt, collect 200 items for "Krypt Keeper", and spend the first hour grabbing every fatality token you pass. The tokens unlock the fatality trophies later, and hunting them separately is the single most common wasted hour in this list.

TrophyRequirement
Grave RobberFirst 10 Krypt items.
Krypt Keeper200 items. Around 2 hours with the map route.
Gear HeadEquip a full gear set on one fighter.
source: PlayStationTrophies.org list, attributed. not copied.

Towers, fatalities, grind

From here the order stops mattering. Clear towers while farming fatalities in the same sessions, then let the online counters accumulate. "Tower Time" is the only seasonal watch: check the tower list before the season turns.

TrophyRequirement
Fatal AttractionAny fatality. Needs a token from the Krypt.
Fatality Fiction10 different fatalities.
Tower Time MISSABLEClear the season's towers before rotation.
Krowd Favorite50 online matches, wins not required.
Elder GodEverything above. Pops automatically.

Where the route went. In this shape the order lives inside the prose and section sequence. It is readable, printable and honest. What it is not is tickable: nothing here marks where you are, and the missables only protect you if you happened to read the right paragraph at the right time.
